## 中文

- 新增可选的自动 SNT 读取，在软件启动及硬盘接入时获取空闲 USB 硬盘的健康信息。
- 保留最近有效数据，忙碌时等待，反复读取失败后暂停；手动读取成功后恢复后续自动尝试。
- 设置显示设备服务状态警示，点击红色感叹号查看提示，服务不可用时禁用自动读取开关。

下载 `Capricorn-v3.2.0-macOS.zip` 并安装固定名称 `Capricorn.app`。V2.10.2 及以后支持应用内更新；更早版本及旧开发 Build `1000` 需手动安装。

“设置 > 硬盘操作 > 自动读取 SNT”默认关闭。先启用特权设备服务并完成系统批准及所需卷访问授权，再打开此开关。开启后无需逐盘或逐次确认；每次软件启动或硬盘接入成功读取一次，不持续轮询健康数据。手动读取入口继续保留。

占用或无法完整检查占用时约 30 秒后复查，不计读取失败次数；可重试读取最多连续失败三次，首次及第二次失败后分别等待 30 秒和 2 分钟。未知桥接器的三种协议均读取失败后停止自动尝试。停止状态跨启动保留，手动成功可覆盖旧结论并清零失败次数；缓存数据保留原始读取时间。

**SNT 会临时卸载该 USB 设备上的全部卷，并尝试恢复挂载。** 请先关闭相关文件、结束其他磁盘任务并备份重要数据。不会强制卸载或结束占用程序；恢复挂载不确定时暂停自动操作，可能需要重新连接或使用“磁盘工具”。关闭开关停止后续尝试，已接管设备的服务仍会完成释放和恢复流程。

特权设备服务推荐在 `/Applications` 启用，也可使用本机可写、非可移除磁盘上的固定文件夹。服务未启用或连接异常时自动读取开关置灰；点击旁边的红色感叹号查看启用或重新启用服务的提示。手动移动或替换 App 前先停用服务；应用内更新尝试恢复此前已启用的服务，系统仍可能要求重新批准。可移动卷隐私授权由 macOS 单独管理。

兼容性取决于桥接器、固件与设备，不保证全部 USB 设备可用，也不提供通用 SD 卡健康读取。常规读取使用内置 smartctl 7.5，SAT SMART Drive 可选。测速与负载使用所选文件夹中的临时文件，请确认目标并保留备份。

## English

- Adds optional automatic SNT reads for idle USB disks on app launch and connection.
- Retains the last valid reading, waits while disks are busy, and pauses repeated failures; a successful manual read enables future automatic attempts.
- Adds clickable device-service warnings in Settings and disables the automatic-read switch while the service is unavailable.

Download `Capricorn-v3.2.0-macOS.zip` and install the stable-name `Capricorn.app`. V2.10.2 and later support in-app upgrades; earlier versions and old development Build `1000` require manual installation.

Settings > Disk Actions > Read SNT automatically is off by default. Enable the privileged device service, complete system approval and grant required volume access before enabling it. Automatic reads require no per-disk or per-read confirmation and retrieve health once per launch or connection, rather than continuously polling health. Manual reads remain available.

Busy or incompletely inspected disks are checked again after approximately 30 seconds without consuming failures. Retryable reads stop after three consecutive failures, with delays of 30 seconds and two minutes. An unsuccessful read using all three unknown bridge protocols stops automatic attempts. Exclusions survive relaunch; a successful manual read replaces the conclusion and resets failures. Cached data retain their original reading time.

**SNT temporarily unmounts every volume on the USB device and attempts to restore mounts.** Close files, finish disk tasks and back up important data first. Disks are never force-unmounted and occupying processes are not terminated. Uncertain restoration pauses automatic operations and may require reconnecting the device or using Disk Utility. Turning the switch off prevents further attempts while an existing worker completes release and restoration.

Enable the privileged device service from `/Applications` or another permanent folder on a local writable, non-removable disk. The automatic-read switch is disabled when the service is off or unhealthy; click its red warning icon for activation or recovery guidance. Disable the service before manually moving or replacing the app. In-app updates attempt to restore a previously enabled service; macOS may require renewed approval. Removable-volume privacy consent is managed separately by macOS.

Compatibility depends on the bridge, firmware and device; not every USB device is supported, and general SD-card health queries are not provided. Ordinary reads use bundled smartctl 7.5; SAT SMART Drive is optional. Benchmarks and workloads use temporary files in the selected folder. Verify targets and keep backups.

本次自动化验证使用模拟磁盘和命令，未执行真实 USB 接管、卷卸载或硬件自检。未完整验证真实设备上的隐私权限撤销及再次授权、全系统占用可见性、接管中拔盘、多卷恢复及其他 Mac 的服务批准。卷可访问检查不能替代系统隐私授权状态 API，不承诺所有权限变化场景均无系统弹窗。Intel 与 macOS 14 仍需对应环境验收。

Automated SNT tests use simulated disks and commands; no real USB capture, volume unmount or hardware self-test was performed. Real-device privacy revocation and renewed consent, system-wide occupancy visibility, unplugging during capture, multivolume restoration and service approval on other Macs remain acceptance gaps. Volume-access checks are not a system privacy-authorization-status API, and absence of system prompts after every permission change is not guaranteed. Intel and macOS 14 require separate validation.

安装回归使用隔离测试 App、历史及偏好，不替换真实用户 App 或数据库。服务衔接场景使用模拟协调器，不等于真实 root 服务跨版本安装或授权保留验收。

Installation regressions use isolated fixture apps, history and preferences without replacing the real user app or database. The service-handoff scenario uses a mock coordinator and does not establish real root-service upgrades or retained system approval.
