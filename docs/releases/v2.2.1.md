## 中文

Capricorn V2.2.1 使用 `Version = 2.2`、`Build = 1` 和 Display Name `Capricorn V2.2.1`。本版将多分区磁盘的显示与文件操作统一到一个明确且可持久化的代表卷，并避免把 APFS 结构卷误作为普通数据卷。

### 主要更新

- 外接物理磁盘可在“磁盘操作 > 切换卷宗”中选择代表卷。侧边栏、重命名、测速和实时活动自动负载都使用该选择；系统盘和网络卷不支持切换。
- 设置新增启动卷策略：默认容量最大的可操作卷，或恢复上次手动选择。选择优先使用硬件序列号和卷 UUID 持久化，避免重新插拔后 `diskN` 变化。
- 过滤 EFI、APFS 容器支撑分区和 Preboot、Recovery、VM、Update 等 APFS 辅助卷。系统盘固定优先根挂载卷，显示 `Macintosh HD` 而非 `Preboot`。
- 扩充 Intel/Solidigm 数据中心 SSD 和 WD Ultrastar DC SN640 的型号映射，涵盖 D5、D7、PCIe 5.0、EDSFF 与 U.2 变体。

### 下载与兼容性

- Release 包名：`Capricorn-v2.2.1-macOS.zip`
- App 名称：`Capricorn V2.2.1.app`
- 系统要求：macOS 14.0 或更新版本
- 可选依赖：用户自行安装的 smartmontools (`smartctl`) 与 SAT SMART Driver
- 本版按要求跳过签名和公证；首次打开可能需要通过 Finder > 打开或“系统设置 > 隐私与安全性”手动允许。

## English

Capricorn V2.2.1 uses `Version = 2.2`, `Build = 1`, and Display Name `Capricorn V2.2.1`. This release unifies multi-volume disk display and file operations around a clear, persistent representative volume, while keeping APFS structural volumes out of ordinary data-volume workflows.

### Highlights

- External physical disks can choose a representative volume through Disk Actions > Switch Volume. The sidebar, rename action, benchmark targets, and automatic Live Activity workloads follow that choice; system and network disks cannot switch.
- Settings adds a startup-volume policy: choose the largest safe volume by default or restore the last manual selection. The preference persists primarily through hardware serial and volume UUID, so it survives a changing `diskN` identifier after reattachment.
- Filters EFI, APFS container backing partitions, and APFS support volumes such as Preboot, Recovery, VM, and Update. System disks explicitly prefer the root-mounted volume and display `Macintosh HD` instead of `Preboot`.
- Expands Intel/Solidigm data-center SSD and WD Ultrastar DC SN640 model recognition across D5, D7, PCIe 5.0, EDSFF, and U.2 variants.

### Download And Compatibility

- Release package: `Capricorn-v2.2.1-macOS.zip`
- App name: `Capricorn V2.2.1.app`
- Requires macOS 14.0 or later
- Optional user-installed smartmontools (`smartctl`) and SAT SMART Driver
- Signing and notarization were intentionally skipped as requested. First launch may require Finder > Open or approval in System Settings > Privacy & Security.
