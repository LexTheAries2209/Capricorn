## 中文

- 48 小时内已有有效 SNT 数据时跳过自动读取，保留手动更新；过期后读取失败仍显示上次数据。
- SNT SMART 表格和历史报告显示实际采集时间，CSV 保留对应时间字段。
- 调整 SMART 默认列宽，名称列缩小 15%，为 ID 留出更多空间。

下载 `Capricorn-v3.2.1-macOS.zip` 并安装固定名称 `Capricorn.app`。V2.10.2 及以后支持应用内更新；更早版本及旧开发 Build `1000` 需手动安装。

自动 SNT 默认关闭。先启用设备服务，完成系统批准和所需卷访问授权，再开启自动读取。启动或插入硬盘时，如果成功采集的数据未满 48 小时，直接使用缓存；满 48 小时后才尝试自动读取。持续连接期间不会因缓存到期主动读取。失败或占用不会延长有效期，旧数据保留真实采集时间；手动成功后重新计算 48 小时。

占用或无法完整检查占用时约 30 秒后复查，不计读取失败次数。连续三次有效失败或三种未知桥接协议全部失败会停止自动尝试；手动成功可以恢复。挂载恢复不确定时暂停自动操作。

**SNT 会临时卸载该 USB 设备上的全部卷，并尝试恢复挂载。** 请先关闭相关文件、结束其他磁盘任务并备份重要数据。不会强制卸载或结束占用程序，可能需要重新连接或使用“磁盘工具”。

推荐在 `/Applications` 或本机可写、非可移除磁盘上的固定文件夹启用服务。手动移动或替换 App 前先停用服务；应用内更新尝试恢复此前已启用的服务，系统可能要求重新批准。macOS 单独管理可移动卷隐私授权。

兼容性取决于桥接器、固件与设备，不保证全部 USB 设备可用，不提供通用 SD 卡健康读取。内置 smartctl 7.5，SAT SMART Drive 可选。测速和负载使用所选文件夹中的临时文件，请确认目标并保留备份。

## English

- Reuses valid SNT data for 48 hours while keeping manual refresh available; failed refreshes retain the previous reading.
- Shows the actual capture time in the SNT SMART table and history reports, with the corresponding timestamp in CSV exports.
- Reduces the default Name column width by 15% to give ID more space.

Download `Capricorn-v3.2.1-macOS.zip` and install the stable-name `Capricorn.app`. V2.10.2 and later support in-app upgrades; earlier versions and old development Build `1000` require manual installation.

Automatic SNT is off by default. Enable the device service, complete system approval and grant required volume access first. On launch or connection, successful readings less than 48 hours old are reused; older data allow an automatic read. Expiry alone does not trigger a read while the disk remains connected. Failed or busy attempts do not extend freshness. Previous data retain their actual capture time, and a successful manual read starts a new 48-hour period.

Busy or incompletely inspected disks are checked again after approximately 30 seconds without consuming failures. Three consecutive effective failures or unsuccessful reads using all three unknown bridge protocols stop automatic attempts; a successful manual read enables future attempts. Uncertain mount restoration pauses automatic operations.

**SNT temporarily unmounts every volume on the USB device and attempts to restore mounts.** Close files, finish disk tasks and back up important data first. Disks are never force-unmounted and occupying processes are not terminated. Reconnecting the device or using Disk Utility may be necessary.

Enable the service from `/Applications` or another permanent folder on a local writable, non-removable disk. Disable it before manually moving or replacing the app. In-app updates attempt to restore a previously enabled service; macOS may require renewed approval. Removable-volume privacy consent is managed separately by macOS.

Compatibility depends on the bridge, firmware and device; not every USB device is supported, and general SD-card health queries are not provided. smartctl 7.5 is bundled; SAT SMART Drive is optional. Benchmarks and workloads use temporary files in the selected folder. Verify targets and keep backups.

自动化 SNT 验证使用模拟磁盘和命令，不执行真实 USB 接管、卷卸载或硬件自检。真实设备权限变化、占用可见性、接管中拔盘、多卷恢复、Intel/macOS 14 及其他 Mac 的服务批准仍需实机验收。卷可访问检查不能替代系统隐私授权状态 API。

Automated SNT tests use simulated disks and commands without real USB capture, volume unmount or hardware self-tests. Real-device permission changes, occupancy visibility, unplugging during capture, multivolume restoration, Intel/macOS 14 and service approval on other Macs require separate validation. Volume-access checks are not a system privacy-authorization-status API.

安装回归使用隔离测试 App、历史及偏好，不替换真实用户 App 或数据库；服务衔接使用模拟协调器，不等于真实 root 服务跨版本授权保留验收。

Installation regressions use isolated fixture apps, history and preferences without replacing the real user app or database. The mock service-handoff coordinator does not establish real root-service upgrades or retained system approval.
