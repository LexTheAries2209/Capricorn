## 中文

- 更新提示统一为紧凑中文窗口，内容居中、按句子换行，修复等待圈残留和确认按钮无响应。
- 启动检查 15 秒无结果自动结束，等待下次启动或手动重试；安装包下载与安装不受此限制。
- 优化设置标题和更新状态布局，应用菜单与设置新增第三方许可入口。

下载 `Capricorn-v2.10.3-macOS.zip`，解压后安装 `Capricorn.app`。V2.10.2 可使用应用内更新；V2.10.1 及更早版本与旧开发 Build `1000` 需手动安装。

清单和更新包继续使用原有 Ed25519 密钥验证，下载遵循系统代理。App 为 ad-hoc 签名，没有 Developer ID 或 Apple 公证，首次启动可能需要手动允许。自动检查与自动下载默认关闭，已有选择会保留。有冲突任务时阻止更新，安装前保存历史；内置 smartctl 7.5。

## English

- Unifies update prompts with compact localized windows, centered content, and sentence-aware line breaks; fixes lingering spinners and unresponsive confirmation buttons.
- Ends unanswered launch checks after 15 seconds until the next launch or manual retry, without limiting update downloads or installation.
- Improves the settings title and update status layout, and adds third-party license access in the application menu and Settings.

Download `Capricorn-v2.10.3-macOS.zip` and install `Capricorn.app`. V2.10.2 supports in-app updates; V2.10.1 and earlier, and old development Build `1000`, require manual installation.

The feed and archive retain verification with the existing Ed25519 key, and downloads follow system proxy settings. The App is ad-hoc signed without Developer ID or Apple notarization; first launch may require manual approval. Checks and automatic downloads are off by default, preserving saved choices. Conflicting tasks block updates, history is saved before installation, and smartctl 7.5 is bundled.
