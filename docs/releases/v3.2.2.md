## 中文

- 自动 SNT 跳过只读介质及包含只读卷的硬盘，减少接管后挂载恢复失败的风险。
- 将“读取错误日志”移至开发者选项，默认关闭，同时受开发者选项总开关控制。
- 新增默认关闭的“始终显示 SMART 自检”。启用后，对已有 SMART 信息的设备放宽自检界面显示条件；SNT 硬盘仍不显示。关闭时沿用原有规则，实际自检命令的安全限制保持不变。

下载 `Capricorn-v3.2.2-macOS.zip` 并安装固定名称 `Capricorn.app`。V2.10.2 及以后支持应用内更新；更早版本及旧开发 Build `1000` 需手动安装。

自动 SNT 默认关闭。先启用设备服务，完成系统批准和所需卷访问授权，再开启自动读取。自动探测只考虑可写介质且所有已知卷均可写的硬盘；手动 SNT 仍需用户主动操作。48 小时内的有效数据会复用，持续连接期间不会因缓存到期主动读取。占用时等待空闲，连续失败或挂载恢复不确定时暂停自动尝试。

**手动或自动 SNT 会临时卸载该 USB 设备上的全部卷，并尝试恢复挂载。** 请先关闭相关文件、结束其他磁盘任务并备份重要数据。不会强制卸载或结束占用程序，可能需要重新连接或使用“磁盘工具”。本版没有开放 SNT 的自检或错误报告界面，也不把硬件实验结果当作通用支持。

推荐从 `/Applications` 或本机可写、非可移除磁盘上的固定目录启用服务。手动移动或替换 App 前先停用服务；应用内更新尝试恢复此前已启用的服务，系统可能要求重新批准。兼容性取决于桥接器、固件与设备；不提供通用 SD 卡健康读取。

## English

- Skips automatic SNT reads for read-only media and disks containing read-only volumes, reducing the risk of mount-restoration failures.
- Moves the error-log reader to Developer Options, off by default and gated by the developer master switch.
- Adds the off-by-default “Always show SMART self-tests” option. It relaxes display conditions for devices with SMART information while continuing to exclude SNT disks. Disabling it preserves the previous rules; command safety restrictions remain unchanged.

Download `Capricorn-v3.2.2-macOS.zip` and install the stable-name `Capricorn.app`. V2.10.2 and later support in-app upgrades; earlier versions and old development Build `1000` require manual installation.

Automatic SNT is off by default. Enable the device service, complete system approval and grant required volume access first. Automatic probing requires writable media and writable known volumes; manual SNT still requires an explicit user action. Valid readings are reused for 48 hours, and expiry alone does not trigger a read while a disk remains connected. Busy disks wait until idle; repeated failures or uncertain mount restoration pause automatic attempts.

**Manual and automatic SNT temporarily unmount every volume on the USB device and attempt to restore mounts.** Close files, finish disk tasks and back up important data first. Disks are never force-unmounted and occupying processes are not terminated. Reconnecting the device or using Disk Utility may be necessary. This release does not enable SNT self-test or error-report panels, and hardware experiments do not establish general support.

Enable the service from `/Applications` or another permanent folder on a local writable, non-removable disk. Disable it before manually moving or replacing the app. In-app updates attempt to restore a previously enabled service; macOS may require renewed approval. Compatibility depends on the bridge, firmware and device; general SD-card health queries are not provided.

Automated SNT validation uses simulated disks and commands without real USB capture. A prior explicitly authorized manual CODEX experiment read an error log with no valid returned entries. That device reported no self-test support; no self-test records were obtained and no hardware self-test was started. This evidence applies only to that device and bridge and does not establish a released SNT log feature. Real multivolume restoration, Intel/macOS 14, service approval on other Macs and retained root-service approval across upgrades require separate validation. Installation regressions use isolated apps and data, with a mock service-handoff coordinator.
