# Capricorn V2.2.2 Release Notes

Release date: 2026-08-25

## Highlights

- Redesigns volume filtering around diskutil APFS Physical Store relationships, partition content, and actual operability instead of relying on BSD names such as `disk11s2`.
- Capacity at or below 1 GB is only a supporting signal when topology data is incomplete. A legitimate mounted filesystem with a volume UUID remains visible, switchable, and available for benchmarking.
- APFS container backing stores, EFI, and other structural partitions stay out of Overview, the sidebar representative volume, Switch Volume, benchmarks, Live Activity workloads, checks and repairs, and SMART CSV volume metadata.
- Capacity and filesystem summaries use only user-visible volumes, avoiding duplicated container capacity or structural formats.

## Download And Compatibility

- Release package: `Capricorn-v2.2.2-macOS.zip`
- App name: `Capricorn V2.2.2.app`
- Requires macOS 14.0 or later
- Optional user-installed smartmontools (`smartctl`) and SAT SMART Driver
- As confirmed for this release, the package carries a local Apple Development signature but no Developer ID signature or notarization. First launch may require Finder > Open or approval in System Settings > Privacy & Security.

## Safety Notes

- Capricorn creates benchmark or workload temporary files only inside the selected folder and does not write directly to raw devices.
- Write, mixed, loop, and large-file workloads can stress storage. Confirm that the target volume belongs to the intended disk.
- Partitions identified as storage topology or metadata are never offered as file-operation targets.
