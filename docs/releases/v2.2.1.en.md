# Capricorn V2.2.1 Release Notes

Release date: 2026-08-25

## Highlights

- Multi-volume disks now use a representative volume as the common basis for the sidebar label, rename actions, benchmark targets, and automatic Live Activity workloads. External physical disks can select an operable volume from Disk Actions > Switch Volume; system and network disks cannot switch, while Time Machine, read-only, and unmounted volumes remain visible but unavailable.
- Settings adds “Volume selected when Capricorn opens.” The default is the largest safe volume; it can instead restore the last manual selection. Selections prefer the complete hardware serial number and volume UUID so a reattached device does not depend on a changing `diskN` name.
- EFI, unmounted APFS container backing partitions, and APFS support volumes such as Preboot, Recovery, VM, and Update no longer appear as ordinary volumes or operation targets. A system disk explicitly resolves to its root-mounted volume, so it displays `Macintosh HD` rather than `Preboot`.
- The model catalog adds Intel/Solidigm enterprise SSD mappings for D5-P4320, D5-P4326, D5-P4420, D5-P5430, D5-P5336, D7-P4510, D7-P4511, D7-P4610, D7-P5810, D7-PS1010, and D7-PS1030, plus WD Ultrastar DC SN640. Several EDSFF and U.2 part-number variants are also covered.

## Download And Compatibility

- App name: `Capricorn V2.2.1.app`
- Requires macOS 14.0 or later
- Optional user-installed smartmontools (`smartctl`) and SAT SMART Driver
- Signing and notarization were intentionally skipped as requested. First launch may require Finder > Open or approval in System Settings > Privacy & Security.

## Safety Notes

- Capricorn creates benchmark or workload temporary files only inside the chosen folder and does not write directly to raw devices.
- Write, mixed, loop, and large-file workloads can stress storage. Confirm that the representative volume or manual target is on the intended disk.
- System, network, Time Machine, read-only, and unmounted volumes are never selected as representative-volume operation targets.
