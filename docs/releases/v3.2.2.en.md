# Capricorn V3.2.2

Release date: November 11, 2026

Complete Version `3.2.2` · Build `0057` · macOS 14.0+ · arm64 / x86_64

Build is an independent, globally increasing four-digit release-build sequence and is not appended to the version.

## Changes

- Skips automatic SNT reads for read-only media and disks containing read-only volumes, reducing the risk of mount-restoration failures on media such as Codex cards.
- Moves the error-log reader to Developer Options, off by default and gated by the developer master switch.
- Adds the off-by-default “Always show SMART self-tests” option. It bypasses the usual panel-display conditions for devices with SMART information, while continuing to exclude SNT disks and devices without SMART information. Disabling it preserves the previous rules. Command safety restrictions and error-log visibility conditions remain unchanged.

## Installation And Use

Download `Capricorn-v3.2.2-macOS.zip` and place the stable-name `Capricorn.app` in `/Applications` or another permanent local tools folder. V2.10.2 and later support in-app upgrades; earlier versions and old development Build `1000` require one manual installation.

Automatic SNT is off by default. Enable the device service, complete system approval and grant required volume access first. Automatic probing requires writable media and writable known volumes. Valid SNT readings are reused for 48 hours; expiry alone does not initiate a read while a disk remains connected. Busy disks wait, and repeated failures or uncertain mount restoration pause automatic attempts. Manual reads remain available as explicit actions.

SNT temporarily unmounts every volume on the USB device and attempts to restore mounts. Disks are never force-unmounted and occupying processes are not terminated. Close files, finish disk tasks and back up important data first. Reconnection or Disk Utility may be needed after failed restoration. SNT self-test and error-report panels remain unavailable in this release.

Enable the service from a permanent folder on a local writable, non-removable disk. Disable it before manually moving or replacing the app. In-app updates attempt to restore a previously enabled service; macOS may require renewed approval. Removable-volume privacy consent is managed separately. Compatibility depends on the bridge, firmware and device; not every USB disk is supported, and general SD-card health queries are not provided. smartctl 7.5 is bundled; SAT SMART Drive is optional. Benchmarks and workloads use temporary files in the selected folder.

SNT uses smartmontools PR #639, commit `c68a1b9af76ba9ca3a2e987def6384377f73a4b8`, local patch `capricorn4`, under GPL-2.0-or-later. Capricorn is GPL-3.0-only. See `THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`.

The app, privileged service and tools retain same-team Apple Development signing and the existing Ed25519 update key. Developer ID and Apple notarization are not established; first launch and system approval should not be assumed equivalent to notarized distribution.
