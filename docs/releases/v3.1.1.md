## 中文

- 改善通过 USB 集线器连接设备时的 SMART 与 SNT 识别。
- 无效 SMART 响应和异常序列号不再显示为健康数据，减少错误警报。
- 修正 SMART 目标选择及无法确定设备时的中文提示。

下载 `Capricorn-v3.1.1-macOS.zip` 并安装固定名称 `Capricorn.app`。V2.10.2 及以后支持应用内更新；更早版本及旧开发 Build `1000` 需手动安装。

特权设备服务须在设置中启用并完成系统批准。推荐 `/Applications`，也可使用本机可写、非可移除磁盘上的固定文件夹。手动移动或替换 App 前先停用服务；应用内更新会尝试恢复此前已启用的服务，系统仍可能要求重新批准。

每次实验性 SNT 仍须确认卸载该 USB 设备上的全部卷；请先关闭文件、结束其他磁盘任务并备份重要数据。结束后尝试恢复挂载，失败时可能需要重新连接或使用“磁盘工具”。可移动卷隐私授权由系统单独管理，后台扫描不会主动接管 USB 设备。

本次识别与数据校验修复不会为不支持的设备增加 SMART 能力。未获得可信响应时显示不可用；此前异常属性不能作为设备健康依据。已通过多级集线器下的真实 USB 标识只读核对；本次未执行真实 SNT 接管、卷卸载或硬件自检。桥接器、固件与设备决定兼容性，不保证全部 USB 设备可用，也不提供通用 SD 卡健康读取。

常规读取使用内置 smartctl 7.5，SAT SMART Drive 可选。测速与负载使用所选文件夹中的临时文件，请确认目标并保留备份。

App、特权服务及工具使用同一团队 Apple Development 证书签名，更新包和清单使用既有 Ed25519 密钥。没有 Developer ID 或 Apple 公证；首次打开及系统授权体验不能等同于已公证分发。

## English

- Improves SMART and SNT device identification through USB hubs.
- Rejects invalid SMART responses and malformed serial numbers instead of displaying misleading health data.
- Corrects SMART target selection and the Chinese message for ambiguous devices.

Download `Capricorn-v3.1.1-macOS.zip` and install the stable-name `Capricorn.app`. V2.10.2 and later support in-app upgrades; earlier versions and old development Build `1000` require manual installation.

Enable the privileged device service in Settings and complete system approval. `/Applications` is recommended; other permanent folders on local, writable, non-removable disks are supported. Disable the service before manually moving or replacing the app. In-app updates attempt to restore a previously enabled service; macOS may require renewed approval.

Each experimental SNT read still requires confirmation before unmounting every volume on the USB device. Close files, finish other disk tasks, and back up important data first. Mount restoration is attempted afterward; failure may require reconnecting the device or using Disk Utility. Removable-volume privacy consent remains separate, and background scans never initiate USB capture.

These identification and validation fixes do not add SMART capability to unsupported devices. Unavailable is shown when no trustworthy response is received; previous malformed attributes are not reliable health information. Read-only identity checks passed with a real USB device through nested hubs. No real SNT capture, volume unmount, or hardware self-test was performed for this release. Compatibility depends on the bridge, firmware and device; not every USB device is supported, and general SD-card health queries are not provided.

Ordinary reads use bundled smartctl 7.5; SAT SMART Drive is optional. Benchmarks and workloads use temporary files in the selected folder. Verify targets and keep backups.

The app, privileged service and tools use same-team Apple Development certificate signatures; the archive and feed use the existing Ed25519 key. Developer ID and Apple notarization are not established. First launch and system approval should not be assumed equivalent to notarized distribution.
