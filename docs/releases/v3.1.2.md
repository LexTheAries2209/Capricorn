## 中文

- 改善磁盘短暂正忙时的 SNT 读取，加入有限次卸载重试。
- 无法卸载时显示更清晰的提示及可能占用磁盘的程序，便于释放磁盘后重试。

下载 `Capricorn-v3.1.2-macOS.zip` 并安装固定名称 `Capricorn.app`。V2.10.2 及以后支持应用内更新；更早版本及旧开发 Build `1000` 需手动安装。

特权设备服务须在设置中启用并完成系统批准。推荐 `/Applications`，也可使用本机可写、非可移除磁盘上的固定文件夹。手动移动或替换 App 前先停用服务；应用内更新会尝试恢复此前已启用的服务，系统仍可能要求重新批准。

每次实验性 SNT 仍须确认卸载该 USB 设备上的全部卷；请先关闭文件、结束其他磁盘任务并备份重要数据。结束后尝试恢复挂载，失败时可能需要重新连接或使用“磁盘工具”。重试不会强制卸载磁盘或结束占用程序，显示的程序仅是排查线索。可移动卷隐私授权由系统单独管理，后台扫描不会主动接管 USB 设备。

兼容性取决于桥接器、固件与设备，不保证全部 USB 设备可用，也不提供通用 SD 卡健康读取。本次未在真实 SanDisk 设备上复现占用故障，未执行真实 SNT 接管、卷卸载或硬件自检。

常规读取使用内置 smartctl 7.5，SAT SMART Drive 可选。测速与负载使用所选文件夹中的临时文件，请确认目标并保留备份。

签名及发布验证结果见下文；不宣称 Developer ID 或 Apple 公证。

## English

- Improves SNT reads when a disk is temporarily busy with bounded unmount retries.
- Shows clearer unmount-failure guidance and a possible occupying process to help users free the disk before retrying.

Download `Capricorn-v3.1.2-macOS.zip` and install the stable-name `Capricorn.app`. V2.10.2 and later support in-app upgrades; earlier versions and old development Build `1000` require manual installation.

Enable the privileged device service in Settings and complete system approval. `/Applications` is recommended; other permanent folders on local, writable, non-removable disks are supported. Disable the service before manually moving or replacing the app. In-app updates attempt to restore a previously enabled service; macOS may require renewed approval.

Each experimental SNT read still requires confirmation before unmounting every volume on the USB device. Close files, finish other disk tasks, and back up important data first. Mount restoration is attempted afterward; failure may require reconnecting the device or using Disk Utility. Retries do not force-unmount disks or terminate processes; the displayed process is only a diagnostic clue. Removable-volume privacy consent remains separate, and background scans never initiate USB capture.

Compatibility depends on the bridge, firmware and device; not every USB device is supported, and general SD-card health queries are not provided. The reported busy state was not reproduced on a real SanDisk device for this release. No real SNT capture, volume unmount, or hardware self-test was performed.

Ordinary reads use bundled smartctl 7.5; SAT SMART Drive is optional. Benchmarks and workloads use temporary files in the selected folder. Verify targets and keep backups.

Signing and release verification are recorded below; Developer ID and Apple notarization are not claimed.
